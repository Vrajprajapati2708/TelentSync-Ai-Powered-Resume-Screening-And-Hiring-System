The TalentSync Story

We believe hiring should be fair, fast, and human.

Manual resume screening is broken. It's biased, time-consuming, and leaves great talent undiscovered. TalentSync was built to fix this by empowering HR teams with transparent AI.

Our Mission

We are on a mission to democratize the recruitment process. By leveraging Natural Language Processing and Machine Learning, we remove human fatigue and unconscious bias from the initial screening phase.

Every candidate deserves to be evaluated on their actual skills and experience, not on how well they format a PDF. Our engine extracts the core competencies that truly matter.

Our Core Principles

Merit Over Metrics

Privacy by Design

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

At TalentSync, we process uploaded PDF resumes to extract professional skills, education, and experience using Natural Language Processing frameworks like spaCy and NLTK.

Data Collection

We collect only the data explicitly provided in the uploaded documents. This text is parsed in-memory. Personal Identifiable Information (PII) is isolated during the TF-IDF scoring phase to ensure unbiased candidate ranking.

Data Retention

Resume data is stored temporarily in our SQLite database for session retrieval. Unless a candidate opts-in for long-term pooling, all parsing artifacts are automatically purged after 30 days.

Terms of Service

Last Updated: May 2026

By accessing the TalentSync API or web application, you agree to these Terms. TalentSync provides mathematical similarity scoring between provided Job Descriptions and Resumes.

Acceptable Use

The service is designed for HR teams and developers. You may not use the API to scrape, reverse-engineer the scoring algorithm, or process illegal documents. API rate limits apply as strictly governed by your active subscription tier.

Cookie Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

TalentSync strongly respects your privacy. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

Essential Cookies Only

We utilize secure, HttpOnly JWT (JSON Web Tokens) stored in browser cookies strictly for authentication and session management. Without these, you cannot log into the dashboard.

GDPR & Compliance

Last Updated: May 2026

TalentSync is built with GDPR compliance at its core. We act as a Data Processor for the resumes you upload.

  • Right to Erasure: Candidates or HR teams can instantly purge parsed profiles from the database via the UI.
  • Data Portability: Extracted JSON profiles can be exported at any time.
  • Processing Location: All NLP parsing occurs on encrypted servers.
